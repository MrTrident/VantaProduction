# VANTA PRODUCTION

> **CREATIVITY. BUILT TOGETHER.**  
> An independent creative production network bringing talented artists and creators together.

Vanta Production is a full-scale, premium, production-ready website and private creative production management platform built with Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Supabase.

---

## Ecosystem Overview

1. **Premium Public Website**: Cinematic aesthetic, dark obsidian palette, bold typography, and interactive parallax floating artwork system.
2. **Public Artist Directory & Individual Portfolios**: Unique `/artists/[username]` URLs supporting customizable layouts (Grid, Masonry, Editorial), themes, and availability indicators.
3. **Private Artists Hub (`/dashboard`)**: Dedicated artist workspace for uploading 4K deliverables, tracking moderation decisions, managing drafts, and styling appearance.
4. **Complete Administrative Platform (`/admin`)**: 17 dedicated operational sections covering organization charts, staff appointments, departments, custom role builder, portfolio moderation, recruitment, creator CMS, and audit logging.
5. **Organizational Hierarchy & Positions**: Interactive visual hierarchy chart supporting 15 default position tiers and unlimited custom positions with strict Founder Protection.
6. **Custom Role Builder & Granular Permissions**: Comprehensive permission matrix spanning 12 categories with scopes (`global`, `department`, `team`, `assigned`, `own`).
7. **Two-Method Artist Recruitment**: Direct tokenized invitations (Method A) and public application screening (Method B via `/apply`).
8. **Dynamic Homepage CMS**: Live control over hero copy, floating artwork count/speed/intensity, and section toggles directly from Admin.
